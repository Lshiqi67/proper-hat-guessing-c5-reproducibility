\subsection{An explicit enumeration procedure}
The following finite procedure is sufficient to reconstruct every count
representative without trusting the original list.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For each $n=17,18,\ldots,25$, enumerate all
$(e_0,\ldots,e_4)\in\mathbb Z_{\geq0}^5$ with total $2n-30$.
\item Compute $b_j=6+e_j$ and the candidate $a$ by~\eqref{eq:inverse}.
Reject a nonintegral candidate or one failing~\eqref{eq:A1}--\eqref{eq:A4}.
\item Form the five shifts of $a$ and the five shifts of its reversal.
Retain their minimum under~\eqref{eq:canonical}; remove duplicate minima.
\item Sort the representatives by their five-tuples (ordinary lexicographic
order), compare with \path{profiles.json}, and check
the corresponding row of Table~\ref{tab:counts}.
\end{enumerate}
Formula~\eqref{eq:inverse} proves that no candidate satisfying the count
conditions is missed. For the refinement, enumerate all words in
$\{1,2,3,4\}^5$, replace each word by its first-occurrence labels, remove
duplicates, and compare the resulting 51 words with the stored list.

\subsection{What an independent replay must establish}
An independent replay should verify the following logically separate
requirements.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Reconstruct the 125 one-hot answer choices and all 1020 proper
configurations, and match the exact base formula of every parent input.
\item Check the semantic identity of each of the five groups of 16 count
variables, the intended count, the auxiliary-variable boundaries, and the
absence of unlisted constraints. Establish~\eqref{eq:extension} for every
actual component type by exhaustive input substitution and direct clause
evaluation, or by a separately justified encoder proof.
\item Reconstruct both symmetry actions, the representative list, and
every selected 51-way refinement. Confirm that a child has exactly the
parent clauses and its specified diagonal units.
\item Read every adopted proof, verify every addition by unit propagation,
and require a checked empty clause. Ignoring all deletion records is
permitted by Lemma~\ref{lem:rup}; accepting an unchecked addition is not.
\item Check that all 1413 parents are covered by the 8613 terminal
certificates, with no unknown or unverified terminal case. Verify the
four-color lower bound separately, either by the displayed construction
or by direct evaluation of all 240 configurations.
\end{enumerate}

The recorded audit scripts implement these checks, but a rerun that merely
accepts stored cache reports is not a fresh verification of the proof
steps. For a genuinely fresh replay, each adopted input and proof must be
passed directly to a checker. In the recorded implementation a terminal
call has the form
\begin{center}\small\ttfamily
RupCheckNative.exe instance.cnf proof.drup
\end{center}
and the alternative fast checker has the same arguments. A zero exit code
is accepted only together with the checked-empty-clause report. A new
implementation or a separately verified proof checker would give a
stronger independence guarantee than replaying the same executable.

\subsection{Bookkeeping boundaries}
Raw solver statuses, interrupted runs, and later replacements are kept
separate. In particular, some parent reports remain \texttt{UNKNOWN}
because their exclusion was subsequently obtained from all their refined
children. Some control-program completion reports were not updated after
a standard-output encoding error, although the independent mathematical
audit had already written its completed report. These events are recorded
separately; they are not treated as refutations. The final selected-input
and certificate manifest, together with the complete per-weight audit,
identifies the evidence actually used in the proposed proof.

Source and document snapshots do not duplicate all large certificate
files. Reproduction therefore requires the original input and certificate
directories as well as the manuscript sources and manifest. No claim is
made that the present PDF, on its own, constitutes the complete machine
certificate archive.

\path{checks/check_mathematics.py} in the preserved

implementation. Fresh proof replay covered the three weight-17 leaves
